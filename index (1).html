Turn 03 The grid

Came for the code.
Stayed for the Fed.

I came to Purdue from Ahmedabad in 2023 as a CS major, convinced I'd spend my career building software. Then I took macroeconomics.

Watching a single Fed decision move markets made me realize I didn't just want to build systems — I wanted to understand the systems they operate in. So I added Economics.

Since then my work has lived at the intersection: using code and data to understand markets, businesses, and the decisions inside them.
